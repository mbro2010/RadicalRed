Where the numbers come from. Stats, types, abilities, moves, learnsets, evolutions, wild encounters, trainers and every boss team are read from the Radical Red 4.1 ROM. Damage uses the open-source Smogon damage calculator (Gen 9 rules) fed with those values. Sprites are decoded from your own copy of the game.

Battle plans. Each fight's plan simulates the whole battle hundreds of times, double battles as doubles: damage rolls, crits, misses, speed, status, confusion, stat changes, hazards, weather, terrain, healing, berries, Mega Evolution, Z-Moves and other key items and abilities. The boss AI is ported from the battle AI of the engine Radical Red is built on (Complete Fire Red Upgrade): the same move scores, fighting styles and switching rules. Radical Red may have private changes to it that the public source doesn't show.

Limits. Radical Red's own abilities and moves are modeled from their in-game descriptions and flagged when an effect isn't calculated. Double battles are planned one turn ahead. The order of routes and fights follows the story; if you play them in another order, log any area or fight from the step you're on.
