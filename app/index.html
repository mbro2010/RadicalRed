Where the numbers come from. Stats, types, abilities, moves, learnsets, evolutions, wild encounters, trainers and every boss team are read from the Radical Red 4.1 ROM. Damage uses the open-source Smogon damage calculator (Gen 9 rules) fed with those values. Sprites are decoded from your own copy of the game.

Battle plans. The Plan tab simulates the whole fight hundreds of times: damage rolls, crits, misses, speed, status, confusion, stat changes, hazards, weather, terrain, healing, berries, Mega Evolution, Z-Moves and other key items and abilities. The boss AI is ported from the battle AI of the engine Radical Red is built on (Complete Fire Red Upgrade): the same move scores, fighting styles and switching rules. Radical Red may have private changes to it that the public source doesn't show.

Limits. Double battles are planned as singles. Radical Red's own abilities and moves are modeled from their in-game descriptions and flagged when an effect isn't calculated. Your own Z-Moves aren't planned yet.
