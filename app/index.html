Where the numbers come from. Stats, types, abilities, moves, learnsets and every boss team are read from the Radical Red 4.1 ROM. Damage uses the open-source Smogon damage calculator (Gen 9 rules) fed with those values. Risk percentages count every damage roll, multi-hit count, crit and miss, assuming the boss uses its strongest move on you each turn.

Battle plans. The Plan tab simulates the whole fight hundreds of times: damage rolls, crits, misses, speed, status, stat changes, hazards, weather, terrain, healing, berries and other key items and abilities. The boss AI is an approximation of Radical Red's: it takes KOs, otherwise attacks hard, and sets up, statuses, heals, lays hazards or pivots when that looks good.

Limits. Radical Red's own abilities and moves are modeled from their in-game descriptions and flagged when an effect isn't calculated. Confusion, Encore, Taunt and a few rare items aren't simulated. Move tutor moves aren't in move advice yet. Double battles are treated as singles.
