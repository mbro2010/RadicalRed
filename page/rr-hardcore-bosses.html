How Hardcore boss teams work
  • Separate teams. In Hardcore, 153 boss battles use their own team table, not the Normal-mode teams. Normal-mode team lists online don't apply.
  • Boss EVs are zero. Hardcore always turns on Minimal Grinding, which strips EVs from boss Pokémon. The stats below include that.
  • Many boss levels follow yours. When a level shows top −1, that Pokémon's level is set from your highest-level party Pokémon when the battle starts (here, one below it). Stats on this page assume your top Pokémon is at the level cap, the hardest case.
  • Gym leaders use fixed levels that match the Hardcore level cap for that fight.
  • Mega Stones and Z-Crystals appear as held items. Stats shown are before any Mega Evolution.
